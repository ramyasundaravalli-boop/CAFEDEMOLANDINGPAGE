Our Little Corner

Our little corner of happiness.

Maison Noir began with a simple idea — that a great cup of coffee is not just a drink, but a pause. A moment of stillness in a busy day.

We roast in small batches. We bake fresh every morning. We pour slowly, and we pour with care. Everything here is made by hand, made to order, and made to be savoured.
